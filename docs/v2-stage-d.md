# 2.0 stage D: summaries ("החודש שלך", "החודש/השנה שלך בכיס")

Same rules on Android and the website. Not published: owner-testing RC and a Vercel Preview only.

## Rules (`domain/V2Summary.kt` = `web/v2/summary.js`)
Expected results are shared byte-for-byte in `app/src/test/resources/v2-summary.json`. The web copy is checked by
`scripts/check-fixtures.cjs` and generated by `scripts/v2/make-summary-fixtures.cjs` on `claude/v2-web`.

The summary covers a month (`YYYY-MM`) or a year (`YYYY`), and counts only done shifts: planned shifts and future dates never count.

**Amounts**
- Kept per currency; ₪ and $ are never added together.
- Hourly average per currency excludes jobs and shifts with an unknown amount.
- Paid and unpaid are shown separately. Shifts still waiting for a rate are counted.

**Patterns**
- Hours per weekday and per clock hour; a shift that crosses midnight follows across it.
- Top places (up to 5).
- The longest shift.
- Nights: shifts that cross midnight or end at 23:00 or later.

**Trend**
- For the running month, compared with the same days of last month.
- Shown as an insight when the change is at least 5%.

**Up to three insights:** unpaid, waiting for a rate, trend, busiest day.

**Work type ("טיפוס"):**
- ינשוף לילה, ציפור בוקר, לוחם סוף שבוע, המרתוניסט, הנאמן, הלהטוטן.
- A playful comparison of the hours to flights, episodes, films or cups of coffee.

## Screens
**"החודש שלך"** was removed on the owner's request (2026-10-26): no month summary sheet on Home, History or the bell. Home keeps "הכיס של החודש" (per place) without a "סיכום" link. Before its recap opens, the monthly reminder opens that month in History.

**The story recap (month or year):**
- Slides in the "כיס" look: opening, hours, top places, work type, longest shift, weekly rhythm, active hour, bottom line, card.
- Each slide moves on by itself after 6 s. A tap on the left moves forward, on the right back; holding pauses.
- The amount is hidden until "הצג לי".
- The last card (hours, shifts, top place, type, day, longest) never contains amounts.
  - Android shares it as a 1080×1080 picture drawn at share time.
  - The website uses a canvas picture through the share sheet, or a download; text is the last resort.
- On Android the story is drawn over the app itself, not in a separate window.
